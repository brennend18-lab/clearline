A few things to know.

What does my membership include?

Personal limits, activity logging, reflection summaries and a directory of safeguards and support.

Does this connect to my betting accounts?

No. You enter your activity yourself. Clearline does not connect to your bank or betting accounts, place bets or move money.

Is this therapy or a gambling blocker?

No. Clearline provides educational self-reflection tools. Your limits are personal goals, not enforced restrictions. Free support resources can help you explore safeguards and licensed care when needed.

How do I cancel my membership?

The plan is billed monthly and can be canceled anytime. Cancellation stops future renewals. Use My data & account → Manage billing / cancel to stop future renewals.
